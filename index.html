About Me

Muhandis Ahmad Basyasya

I turn moments and experiences into visual stories worth watching.

I’m a videographer and video editor with a strong interest in videography, visual storytelling, and the craft of editing.

Everything I know, I’ve learned on my own, through curiosity, exploration, and hands-on practice. I enjoy taking a moment, an event, or an experience and shaping it into a story people can feel.

  • Willingness to learnGrowing through practice and exploration.
